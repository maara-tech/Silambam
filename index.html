About Our Grandmaster

Our grandmaster has dedicated his life to preserving Silambam, a traditional South Indian martial art. With decades of training, numerous awards, and contributions to spreading Silambam worldwide, he has trained generations of students in the philosophy and techniques of this ancient art form.
